Research and selected engineering work

SAFE RL

GPU-Accelerated Human-Centered Safety Filtering for Autonomous Racing

GPU-parallel policy training, cross-simulator transfer, and online safety filtering.

At Johns Hopkins University, I built a MuJoCo training pipeline, transferred policies to Assetto Corsa through a 48-dimensional semantic interface, and implemented a Q-CBF safety filter around a nominal racing policy and Safety SAC recovery actor.

Vision-Based Humanoid Motion Imitation from Monocular Videos

Monocular video to 3D human motion, G1 retargeting, and reinforcement learning.

  • Recover SMPL motion from RGB video, then retarget it to the Unitree G1 through constrained inverse kinematics.
  • Train an imitation policy in Isaac Lab and evaluate motion playback in simulation and on the physical robot.

Vision-Based Robotic Grasping with GraspNet

Language-guided target selection and RGB-D grasp planning on an RM65 arm.

  • Use Qwen2.5-VL to identify the requested object, then detection and SAM masks to constrain 3D grasp candidates.
  • Filter GraspNet poses for collisions and robot approach-angle limits before real-world grasp execution.

Autonomous Sweeping Robot

STM32F407 and RDK X5 mobile robot with custom PCB and live trajectory visualization.

Integrated embedded motor control, high-level perception, navigation, and hardware bring-up in a complete robot prototype.

Self-Balancing Wheeled Robot

Dual-MCU control, field-oriented motor drive, and cascaded balance control.

Separated state estimation from time-critical FOC motor control and tuned cascaded loops for upright stability and disturbance rejection.

Mobile Manipulation & Control

Inverse kinematics, camera-guided grasping, and STM32 line following and avoidance.

Combined analytical arm kinematics with vision-based target alignment and resource-constrained mobile navigation.
