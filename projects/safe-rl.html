GPU-Accelerated Human-Centered Safety Filtering for Autonomous Racing

GPU-parallel policy training, cross-simulator transfer, and online safety filtering.

At Johns Hopkins University, I built a MuJoCo training pipeline, transferred policies to Assetto Corsa through a 48-dimensional semantic interface, and implemented a Q-CBF safety filter around a nominal racing policy and Safety SAC recovery actor.
