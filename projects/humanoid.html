Vision-Based Humanoid Motion Imitation from Monocular Videos

Monocular video to 3D human motion, G1 retargeting, and reinforcement learning.

  • Recover SMPL motion from RGB video, then retarget it to the Unitree G1 through constrained inverse kinematics.
  • Train an imitation policy in Isaac Lab and evaluate motion playback in simulation and on the physical robot.
